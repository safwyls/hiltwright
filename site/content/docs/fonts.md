Step 2 is Fonts. Here you choose which sound fonts the saber will carry. You pick them from your [font library](font-library.html). You need neither the saber nor its card for this. Nothing is copied yet: the fonts are copied to the card later, on [Card](card.html).

## Choose the fonts

The page has two lists. On the left is **Fonts for (saber)**, the fonts this saber will carry. On the right is your **Font library**, with an **Add** button beside each font.

Each chosen font shows where it stands:

- **On the card**: the saber's card already has it. This shows only when the card has been read.
- **Copied at install**: it is in your library, and Hiltwright will copy it to the card.
- **Missing**: the card was read and does not have it.
- **Not in the library**: Hiltwright has no copy to put on the card. Add it to the library.

The six free fonts that come with Hiltwright are in the library from the start.

## Add a font from this computer

Press **Add .zip** for a font package as you downloaded it, or **Add folder** for a font folder, or a folder that holds several fonts. A font you add is chosen at once. Your original is never changed.

To listen to a font or fix its details, use the [Font library](font-library.html) in the Workshop.

## Fonts the presets use

Every font a preset names counts as chosen. A font that a preset uses cannot be removed from the list: the remove button is off, and its tip says which preset uses it. Change that preset's font first, on [Presets](presets.html).

If you name a font on Presets that is not in the list, it is added.

## The voice pack

With the Fett263 controls, the saber speaks its menus from a voice pack in the card's `common` folder. Below the fonts, the **Voice pack** panel shows the voice this saber will have and the state of the pack on its card:

- **Version 2 on the card**: the card's pack works.
- **Missing**, or an older version: the saber speaks an error on every preset change until the card has a version 2 pack.
- **Mixed with an old pack**: an older pack's files sit beside the new one's, and the saber says "error in font directory" on every preset change.
- **Card not read**: plug the saber in, or put its card in a card reader, and press **Look again**.

Choose another voice from the list to change it. Like the fonts, the pack goes on the card in the trip on [Card](card.html). The trip also fixes a pack that is missing, too old or mixed: the note's **Fix it on the card** button takes you there. With no voice chosen, the one that comes with Hiltwright is put on only when the card has no working pack.

## What happens next

Press **Continue with Presets**. The button is off until at least one font is chosen.

> **Note:** This page only chooses. To put a font on a card that is already installed, without a new install, use [Live › Card](fonts-and-sd.html).
