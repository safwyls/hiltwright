Step 4 is Card. It gets the saber's SD card ready for the next flash: the fonts it should carry, its voice pack, and the presets file when a new preset list is waiting. It works on the saber picked in the [Armory](armory.html), plugged in or not.

The same page is under Live as [Live › Card](fonts-and-sd.html), for a quick fix on a saber that is already flashed. Everything that page does, this one does too: the fonts on the card with their checks, **Preset fonts**, **Add a font**, the tracks, changing the voice, and sharing the card over USB. This page adds the trip below.

## The card, in one trip

Do this with the card out of the saber, in a card reader. A card reader is about 30 times faster than the saber's own USB. In a test, one font took about 4 seconds in a reader and about 120 seconds over the saber's USB.

1. Take the SD card out of the saber and put it in a card reader on this computer. Hiltwright notices it by itself within a few seconds. If it does not, press **Look again**.
2. The note at the top says what the card needs before the flash, and how long it will take. It can list:
   - the fonts you chose on [Fonts](fonts.html) that the card does not have yet;
   - with the Fett263 controls, the voice pack, when the card has no working one (none, too old, or mixed with an older pack's files) or when you chose a different voice on [Fonts](fonts.html);
   - removing the card's presets file, when a new list of presets is waiting. A snapshot of the saber's own presets is kept first.
3. Press **Prepare the card**. Hiltwright does all of it in one go, and remembers the card as this saber's.
4. Put the card back in the saber, plug the saber in, and go on to [Build & Flash](build-and-install.html).

When nothing is left to do, the note says the card is ready for the flash.

Removing the presets file is what lets the new list take effect. Without it, the saber would keep using the presets on its card. Because it is done in the reader, the saber starts with your list after the flash, with no second restart.

If a font you chose is not in your font library, the note says so and links to Fonts. It cannot be copied.

Whatever the voice pack replaces is kept on this computer, and the note says where.

> **Note:** No card reader? With the saber plugged in, press **Share the saber's card**. The trip then works on the shared card the same way, but it is much slower. Give the card back before you flash.
