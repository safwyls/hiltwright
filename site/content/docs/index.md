If you are new here, read the [install guide](install.html), then the [quick start](quick-start.html). The [walkthrough](../walkthrough.html) shows every page in pictures, and the same tour is built into the app: press "Take the tour" at the bottom left of the window.

The pages below follow the app's own sidebar. The Armory is on top: you pick a saber there, then choose what to do with it. **Install** is the five numbered steps that set a saber up: Wiring, Fonts, Presets, Card, and Build & Flash. Nothing reaches the saber until the last one. **Live** is for a saber that is plugged in and already installed: its presets, its controls and its SD card, changed on the saber at once. The workshop is where styles and fonts are kept, built and tried. **Tools** holds Settings and About.
