If you're new here, start with the [install guide](install.html), then the [quick start](quick-start.html). The [walkthrough](../walkthrough.html) shows every page in pictures, and the same tour is built into the app: press "Take the tour" at the bottom left of the window.

The pages below follow the app's own sidebar. The Armory is on top, where you pick a saber and then choose what to do with it. **Install** is the five numbered steps that set a saber up (Wiring, Fonts, Presets, Card, and Build & Flash), and nothing reaches the saber until the last one. **Live** is for a saber that's plugged in and already installed: its presets, its controls and its SD card, changed on the saber right away. The workshop is where styles and fonts are kept, built and tried, and **Tools** holds Settings and About.
